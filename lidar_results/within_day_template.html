Paired-pass diagnostics

Per cart pass: ratio = min(left points, right points) / max(left points, right points), on a 0–1 scale. Calculations use mapped planted positions where a plot key exists. The two field rows may contain different plants; a low ratio is a review flag, not automatically a scanning problem.

Shared-position diagnostics (lowest first)

Only position numbers present on both sides within the same pass. This controls for asymmetric field layouts such as a pass with 4 planted positions in one row but 1 in the other.
